# About DOMCA

*DUTCH OPEN MESHCORE ACTIVITY*

## What is DOMCA?

**DOMCA** — Dutch Open MeshCore Activity — is an initiative to make MeshCore knowledge and documentation accessible to the Dutch community. This website brings together technical guides, background information, and practical guides for everyone interested in off-grid mesh communication.
