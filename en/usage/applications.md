# Practical Applications

*FAMILY · CLUBS · REMOTE STATIONS*

- **Family mesh** — All family members with a MeshCore device in a Room called 'FAMILY-MESH'. Short messages, GPS tracking, and coordination. Works without a mobile network on holidays, festivals, or in remote areas.
- **Morse club** — Club members coordinate CW activities, field days, and spot reports via a shared Room. Members see each other on the map and share real-time QSO information.
- **Amateur Radio Mesh** — On the 70 cm band with callsigns. Digital QSOs, emergency communication, self-routing repeaters. Every node identifies itself in accordance with amateur regulations.
- **Remote Station** — Node on the rooftop with a good antenna, node inside connected via BLE to phone. Control your radio infrastructure via your own mesh — no VPN or internet required.
